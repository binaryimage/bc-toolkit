Legion: binaryimage's choir synth for MPC. Up to 8 singers per note through real vowel formants for bass, tenor, alto and soprano (or Auto by pitch), with Open ("mmm" to "aah"), vowel LFO, vibrato with delay, breath, consonants, Formant shift, a Morph into a synth made from the same voices, sub, ensemble, ping-pong echo and hall. Mod wheel assignable, sustain pedal supported. 5 pages on its own skin, 128 level-matched presets.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Legion` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, make a plugin track, and pick Legion.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
