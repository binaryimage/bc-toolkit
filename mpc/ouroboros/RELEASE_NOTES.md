Ouroboros: binaryimage's chaotic reverser for MPC. Grabs what just played and throws it back backwards on the beat or free. Chaos, Backmask, Swell and Spiral modes, pitch, feedback, reversed-reverb Ghost swells, Freeze and Flip buttons. 2 pages on its own skin, 50 level-matched presets.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Ouroboros` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, and pick Ouroboros as an insert effect.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
