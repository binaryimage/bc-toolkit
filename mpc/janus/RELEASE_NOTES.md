Janus: a 10-voice AN1x-style virtual analogue for Akai MPC standalone hardware.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Janus` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart the MPC, and pick Janus under External VST Plugins.

The one folder has both builds: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key 61, Key 37, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).

See mpc/janus/README.md for the pages, Scenes and presets.
