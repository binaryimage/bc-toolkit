Maggot: binaryimage's noise-but-beautiful mangle synth for MPC. It has morph and fold oscillators, noise, a tuned resonator, a Leviasynth-style weird arp (ratchets, probability, mutate, polymeter length), and live mangling: TAPE STOP, STUTTER, REVERSE, FREEZE, Glitch, Smear, crush, ring mod, wow, and WRECK on the mod wheel. 8 pages on its own textured skin, 100 presets.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Maggot` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, make a plugin track, and pick Maggot.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
