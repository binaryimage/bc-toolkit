Fenrir: binaryimage's brutal distortion for MPC. Two drive stages from six types (tube, fuzz, rectify, fold, diode, crush), Bite, Starve, Howl feedback, noise gate, and a rhythmic Chop gate with 8 patterns locked to the MPC's clock. 2 pages on its own skin, 50 level-matched presets.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Fenrir` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, and pick Fenrir as an insert effect.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
