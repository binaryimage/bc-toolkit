Grendel: binaryimage's tape abomination for MPC. Drive, age, wow, flutter, dropouts, tape eat, crush and hiss, plus a Tape Stop button, a Rewind button and a Possessed knob that fires brakes, backwards grabs, warbles and chewed-tape jumps at random or on the beat. 2 pages on its own skin, 50 level-matched presets.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Grendel` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, and pick Grendel as an insert effect.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
