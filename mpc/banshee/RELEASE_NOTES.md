Banshee: binaryimage's sorrow chorus for MPC. Up to six drifting, detuned chorus voices, a pitched Wail, a moaning vowel Cry, breath noise, flanging feedback and a dark reverb. 2 pages on its own skin, 50 level-matched presets.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Banshee` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, and pick Banshee as an insert effect.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
