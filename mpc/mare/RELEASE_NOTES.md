Mare: binaryimage's nightmare delay for MPC. Ping-pong echoes that drift in pitch, flip backwards, smear and rot a little more on every repeat, with wobble, ducking and a Freeze button. 2 pages on its own skin, 50 level-matched presets.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Mare` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, and pick Mare as an insert effect.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
