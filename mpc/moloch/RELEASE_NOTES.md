Moloch: binaryimage's pitch nightmare for MPC. Up to three pitch-shifted voices jump to random intervals from a scale (chromatic, octaves, fifths, tritones, phrygian, diminished, microtonal) on the beat, glide, and spiral up or down through a Climb feedback loop. Hold and Bend for live abuse. 2 pages on its own skin, 50 level-matched presets.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Moloch` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, and pick Moloch as an insert effect.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
