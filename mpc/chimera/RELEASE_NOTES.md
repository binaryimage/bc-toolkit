Chimera for MPC: binaryimage's effect pedal as an MPC insert effect. Juno-style chorus, triple delay (7 types, serial or parallel), 8-type reverb with freeze, a 4-channel mixer and a 5-band EQ. 18 presets.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Chimera` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, and load Chimera as an insert effect.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
