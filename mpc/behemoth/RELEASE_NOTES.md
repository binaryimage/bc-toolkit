Behemoth: binaryimage's analog-style drum machine for MPC. 12 drums on their own pads (two kicks, snare, clap, rim, three toms, closed and open hats with choke, cymbal, FM drum), each with tune, decay, two character knobs, drive, pan and its own output. Plus the Mother: a DFAM-style voice (two VCOs, FM, sync, noise, ladder filter, decay envelopes) with an 8-step pitch/velocity sequencer you start and stop from pads 14-16. 8 stereo outputs with routing presets, Grit and Glue on the bus. 10 pages on its own skin, 32 level-matched kits.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Behemoth` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, make a plugin track, and pick Behemoth.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
