Golem: binaryimage's ARP 2600-style semi-modular synth for MPC. Three VCOs with sync and cross-FM, a 4012/4072 switchable ladder filter that self-oscillates, ring mod, noise, S&H and lag, ADSR and AR, a boingy spring reverb, Mono or Duo. Every normal of the 2600 is wired, plus 30 colour-coded patch cables you plug in by touch on a jack-panel page, and a 1601-style 16-step sequencer with three CV rows, play/rest/skip/tie, quantize, and Sync/Free/Key modes. 18 pages on its own skin, 64 level-matched presets.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Golem` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, make a plugin track, and pick Golem.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
