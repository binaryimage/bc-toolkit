Dahaka VA for MPC: Two virtual-analog oscillators with 7-voice unison, VA2 hard sync, a sub oscillator and analog drift. It's one head of binaryimage's Dahaka synth, split out on its own. 32 presets, level-matched.

**Install:** MPC OS 3.10.1 or later. On the MPC, go to Preferences > Enhancements (Beta) and turn on Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support. Unzip, copy the `binaryimage - VST - Dahaka VA` folder into `VST2 Plugins` at the root of a drive, restart, make a plugin track, and pick Dahaka VA under External VST Plugins.

Both builds are in the folder: 32-bit (Live, Live II, One, X, Key, Force) and 64-bit (Live III, XL).
